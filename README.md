# Macroeconomic Models Database

Welcome to the Macroeconomic Models Database! This repository serves as a comprehensive collection of macroeconomic models organized by category. Browse through the categories below to explore different types of models.

## Categories

### [Benchmark Models](./Benchmark_Models/)
Standard benchmark models that serve as baselines for macroeconomic analysis.

### [Financial Frictions](./Financial_Frictions/)
Models incorporating financial market imperfections and frictions.

### [Fiscal Policy](./Fiscal_Policy/)
Models focusing on government spending, taxation, and fiscal policy effects.

### [Labor Markets](./Labor_Markets/)
Models analyzing labor market dynamics, unemployment, and wage determination.

### [Open Economy](./Open_Economy/)
Models dealing with international trade, exchange rates, and cross-border flows.

### [Unconventional Policy](./Unconventional_Policy/)
Models exploring non-standard monetary and economic policy tools.

### [Adaptive Learning](./Adaptive_Learning/)
Models incorporating learning dynamics and adaptive expectations.

### [Specialized Sectors](./Specialized_Sectors/)
Models focusing on specific economic sectors or specialized topics.

---

## About

This database is designed to organize and catalog various macroeconomic models for research and educational purposes. Each category contains models with detailed documentation and implementation notes.
